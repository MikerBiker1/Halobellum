| D9 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | "Winner" in the headless runner | For win rates, the side whose survivors would deal more base damage wins the battle (a wipe always wins; equal is a draw). This only affects runner statistics, not match rules. |

| D12 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | Health bars of a selected squad | Health bars show when a unit is damaged (your M2 wording). They also show for every body of the selected squad, even at full health, so you can see the squad you picked. Say if you want them on damaged units only. |
| D13 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | How turning works | Per your request, each unit has a turn time for 180 degrees (starting guideline: chaff 0.25 s, 1-square units 1 s, 2x2-square units 2 s, Titans 3 s). Implemented as: (a) units drive forward along their facing and slow down while they still need to turn, so a unit facing the wrong way stops and turns on the spot first; (b) attacking units turn toward their target at that speed; (c) the **main weapon** only fires once the unit faces its target within **15 degrees** (`mainWeaponFacingToleranceDegrees` in `CombatRules.asset`); other weapons still fire in any direction (D8). Set the tolerance to 180 if you prefer main weapons to act like turrets that never wait for the body to turn. |
| D14 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31, with the acquisition range added (D15) | Squad formation cohesion (details) | Your rule is implemented; these details were my choice: (a) "target another unit" compares enemy **squads**: the formation breaks when its bodies' closest enemies belong to different enemy squads (bodies of one enemy squad count as one target); (b) "collision" means any body touching a body of a different squad, friend or enemy (being pushed apart or nearly touching); (c) "engage in combat" means any body attacking, firing any weapon, or being hit; (d) once broken, a formation stays broken for the rest of the battle; (e) single-body squads (tanks) are never in formation; (f) the formation moves without rotating, heading for the closest enemy its bodies are targeting. Observation: a squad standing exactly between two enemy squads splits at once under (a). If that is too early, (a) could only count once targets are within some distance. |
| D15 | 2026-10-31 | Needs approval | Acquisition range (details) | Your rule is implemented: units only notice enemies within 3 x their range (`acquisitionRangeMultiplier` in `CombatRules.asset`); with nothing in sight they march straight ahead (Team A up the field, Team B down; squads in formation); inside the enemy's deployment zone their acquisition becomes unlimited. Details I chose: (a) each weapon uses its own range x 3, measured to the enemy's edge like weapon range; (b) "enemy zone" starts at the far edge of no man's land; (c) once a unit has entered it, its acquisition stays unlimited for the rest of the battle; (d) a unit whose target dies and sees nothing else marches ahead again; (e) a unit with no possible target at all (for example ground-only weapons against air) stops once it is in the enemy zone. Fixed along the way: two units walking straight at each other now both keep right and pass, instead of blocking each other. |
