# Decisions and proposals

Agents record here any design question, interpretation, or proposed change that needs the human's approval (Bible Section 1). Nothing here changes the design until the human approves it; approved items move into the Bible's decision log (Section 17).

| D1 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | Anti-armor tag | A weapon with the anti-armor tag uses the anti-armor row **instead of** its damage type row (a ballistic anti-armor gun does 1.5x to Heavy, not 0.25 x 1.5). The same applies against shields (anti-armor shield multiplier, see D2). |
| D2 | 2026-10-31 | **Changed by human** 2026-10-31, implemented | Damage to energy shields | Energy shields are a separate layer with their own multipliers, independent of the armor type: Ballistic 0.75, Explosive 1.0, Energy 1.5, Fire 0.5, Melee 1.0, Anti-armor 1.0 (corrected from 0.5 by the human on 2026-10-31). Damage goes to the shield first; once it is down, damage uses the base armor type. If a hit breaks the shield, its remaining base damage carries over to health using the armor multiplier. Values are in `Data/CombatRules.asset`. |
| D3 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | Shield regen rate | Per-unit data field `shieldRegenPerSecond`; the 5 second delay is in `CombatRules.asset`. Values to be set when shielded units (Spartan, Elite, Zealot) are built in M4. |
| D4 | 2026-10-31 | **Changed by human** 2026-10-31, implemented | Arena size | Arena is 60 squares wide. Each army has a 30-square-deep deployment zone, with 2 squares of no man's land between them: 60 x 62 squares in total. Several chaff bodies can share a square within a squad; multi-body squads usually fit in 5 x 2 squares (Mechabellum fits 24 Crawlers in 10 squares). The square size in metres is D10. The deployment grid itself is built in M3. |
| D5 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | Facing in battle | Units start facing the enemy side, face their target while attacking, and face their direction of travel while moving. Squad rotation (default or 90 degrees) only changes the formation's shape. |
| D6 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | Range measurement | Weapon range and minimum range are measured from the shooter's centre to the target's **edge**. |
| D7 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | Splash and blocking | Splash deals full damage to every enemy inside the radius (no falloff) that the attacker's target filter allows (ground splash does not hit air). A projectile blocked by a defense arc is stopped completely, including its splash. Splash victims never block. |
| D8 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | Units with several weapons | The **first weapon is the unit's main weapon**: its target and range decide where the unit moves and when it stops to fight. Each other weapon picks its own closest legal target and fires at anything in its range, even while moving. |
| D9 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | "Winner" in the headless runner | For win rates, the side whose survivors would deal more base damage wins the battle (a wipe always wins; equal is a draw). This only affects runner statistics, not match rules. |
| D10 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31, open to change | Size of one square | The simulation measures distance in metres, so D4 needs a square size. Set to **2 m per square** (arena 120 m x 124 m), in `CombatRules.asset` (`squareSize`). With this size an 18-body chaff squad in formation is about 8.5 m x 4 m, which fits 5 x 2 squares, and the Bible's Marine range 12 reaches 6 squares. A bigger square makes units relatively smaller and ranges relatively shorter. The human may change it at any time by editing `squareSize`; no test pins the value. |
| D11 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | GPU instancing (Section 12) | The Bible says to use LOD models and GPU instancing for large squads. With URP, scene objects are batched by the **SRP Batcher** instead (it takes priority over instancing), and with placeholder art 300 vs 300 runs at about 1 ms per frame. Keep the SRP Batcher; no GPU instancing work before real Halo models are imported. |
| D12 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 | Health bars of a selected squad | Health bars show when a unit is damaged (your M2 wording). They also show for every body of the selected squad, even at full health, so you can see the squad you picked. Say if you want them on damaged units only. |
| D13 | 2026-10-31 | Needs approval | How turning works | Per your request, each unit has a turn time for 180 degrees (starting guideline: chaff 0.25 s, 1-square units 1 s, 2x2-square units 2 s, Titans 3 s). Implemented as: (a) units drive forward along their facing and slow down while they still need to turn, so a unit facing the wrong way stops and turns on the spot first; (b) attacking units turn toward their target at that speed; (c) the **main weapon** only fires once the unit faces its target within **15 degrees** (`mainWeaponFacingToleranceDegrees` in `CombatRules.asset`); other weapons still fire in any direction (D8). Set the tolerance to 180 if you prefer main weapons to act like turrets that never wait for the body to turn. |
