| D23 | 2026-10-31 | **Approved** 2026-10-31 (90%), implemented | Counter web method and threshold | Each unit fights each other unit in single-type armies of about 1800 credits (both totals within 10% of each other), 10 battles per pair with the sides swapped every other battle; win = D9. A unit's counters are the units whose role is in its role's "weak against" list in Section 7 (for anti-air: any ground unit that is not anti-air). It passes if its best counter wins **more than 70%** (M4 wording). Two questions: (1) Section 15 says counters should win **55 to 70%**, while M4 now says **over 70%**. Which one should the balance pass aim for? (2) Battles barely differ between runs (only a 0.3 m random nudge at the start), so most pairs end 10-0 one way or the other. The numbers show which side wins, not by how much. More variation (bigger random placement) would make win rates more informative. **Your answer:** at similar value a counter should win every time (higher tiers, levels and techs from M6 may beat it). **My suggestion:** keep measuring at equal value (armies of about 1800 credits, within 10% of each other) and require the counter to win **at least 9 of 10 battles (90%)**: a true counter wins all of them, and the one spare battle absorbs a fluke from the small random start nudge. Today every unit's best counter wins 100%. If you agree, I set the threshold to 90% and replace Section 15's "counters win 55 to 70%" with it. Optional later (M6): a margin check, e.g. a counter should still win against an army worth 25% more, to show how strong each counter is. On variation: with this goal, the current near-repeatable battles are fine. |
