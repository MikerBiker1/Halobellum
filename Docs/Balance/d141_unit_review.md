# Units after D141, by type and price

The final roster (round p6). D141 balanced the three factions' base units first, in tournaments without exclusive units, then the exclusive units so every sub-faction has a matchup it clearly wins (`Docs/Balance/unit_balance.md`, D141). For each unit:

- **Base only:** ban impact in the base-only tournament (phase 1, round z12: Armored Division, Banished and UNSC-based Flood, every exclusive taken out, 400 matches a pairing, seed 0 compared match by match). Base units only.
- **With exclusives:** ban impact in the full tournament (p6, 100 matches a pairing, seed 0), shifted by each sub-faction's all-seed rate minus its seed-0 rate (a ban re-rolls the matches). The mean over the sub-factions that field it, then each one. Positive = it helps its side.
- **Table:** its average win rate at equal credits against every unit in the AI's counter table (what the Insane AI's buying leans on).
- **Bought:** share of its sub-factions' matches where it fought (8 seeds). **Win:** their win rate in those. **Credits:** its share of their spending.
- Margins: about ±2.5 to ±4.5 for a ban impact; units bought in under 10% of matches weren't ban-tested.
