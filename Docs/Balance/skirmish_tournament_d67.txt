====================================================================
300 Unlock Skirmish matches, where each "sub-faction" below is an AI difficulty: 150 between different difficulties, 150 of a difficulty against itself.
Whole matches under the game's real rules, with towers, cards, techs and spells. Half of each pairing is played with the sides swapped.
Each side is dealt a random starting card for its sub-faction (250 to 350, with base health to match).
Matches with no winner after 60 rounds: 0.
This measures AIs, not good players: a unit the AI uses badly looks weak here. How close numbers must be to mean nothing is given in each table.
