# The balance pass, unit by unit (D138)

Before: the D136 tournament (Insane against Insane, seeds 0 and 1, 1,400 matches a sub-faction). After: the final D138 tournament (seeds 0 to 3, 2,800 matches a sub-faction). Each cell reads before → **after**.

- **Bought:** share of the sub-faction's matches in which it fought.
- **Win:** the sub-faction's win rate in those matches.
- **Edge:** that, minus its win rate without the unit (– when it is bought in nearly every match, so there is too little to compare with). A high edge can also mean the side was ahead.
- **Ratio:** its share of the side's damage over its share of the side's credits (drops, spawns and infected bodies count as its own).
- **Soak:** the same for damage taken. **Died:** share of its bodies dead at a battle's end. **Lvl:** average level of its squads at the match's end.
- Edges and win rates move by about ±3 points between runs this size; ratios by about ±0.1.
