Army slot test: real end-of-match armies from d136_Insane_s0.jsonl, squads at the levels they reached, brought to 4000 credits; a slot is about 800 credits.
A slot comes at the average level its unit reached in that sub-faction's armies (lvl); each level costs half the unit's price, as in a match.
value = points of win rate the unit's slot adds over a slot of the army's own mix (0 = as good as the army's average credit).
margin = the same for the survivors' base damage minus the enemy's, in points of the army's base damage: steadier than the win rate.
±95% = how far a value could be from the truth by chance. in armies = share of the source armies that had the unit. Sorted by margin.
28980 battles in 2.6 minutes; squads that found no room: 0; enemy credits off by 95 on average.
