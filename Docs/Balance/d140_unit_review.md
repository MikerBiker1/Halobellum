# Units after the second pass (D140), by type and price

The final roster (round y5: Insane against Insane, seeds 0 to 3, 2,800 matches a sub-faction). For each unit, over the sub-factions that field it:

- **Ban impact:** how many points of win rate its sub-faction loses when that one unit is taken out of its roster (a ban run on seed 0, compared match by match with the same seed's tournament). Positive = it helps its side; negative = its side does better without it. This is the measure the pass balanced. The round says which balance it was measured on; *changed since* means the unit was tuned after that measurement. Every unit was measured on x4 (the full sweep); the units changed after it were measured again on y1, y4 or y5.
- **Edge:** the side's win rate in matches where it fought, minus in matches where it didn't (– when it is in nearly every match). Shown for comparison: it mixes in when the AI buys a unit, so it barely moves with stats.
- **Bought:** share of its sub-factions' matches where it fought. **Win:** the side's win rate in those. **Credits:** its share of its side's spending.
- **Ratio:** its share of the side's damage over its share of the side's credits. **Soak:** the same for damage taken. **Died:** share of its bodies dead at a battle's end. **Lvl:** its squads' average level at the match's end.
- Margins: a ban impact is good to about ±2.5 points for a unit three sub-factions field, ±4 to ±7 for one that only one fields; edges to about ±3.
