## Three factions

{{factions}}

Each faction has a **base roster** that every player of that faction can unlock, its own **spells**, and its own look in the game's interface. Items, economy cards and army buffs are shared by everyone.

## Sub-factions

Before a match you choose a sub-faction as well. It adds its own **exclusive units** to the base roster, so a Noble player and an Armored Division player share the UNSC base units but nothing else.

{{subfactions}}

> **Note:** The Flood's two sub-factions are built from what the Flood has taken over: UNSC-based brings infested Marines and UNSC vehicles, Covenant-based brings infested Covenant troops and the Spawner.
