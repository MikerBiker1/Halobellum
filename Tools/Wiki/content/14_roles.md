## The nine roles

Every faction has at least one unit in each role. Anti-armor weapons count as Single Target DPS, and there is no separate support role: utility units go in the closest role.

| Role | Its job | Strong against | Weak against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Chaff** | Cheap bodies that soak up shots and swarm | Single Target DPS | Chaff Clear |
| **Chaff Clear** | Splash damage against groups | Chaff | Tanks, Flankers |
| **Single Target DPS** | Heavy damage on one target, anti-armor included | Tanks, heavy units | Chaff swarms, Flankers |
| **Tank** | A tough front line | Chaff Clear | Single Target DPS |
| **Flanker** | Fast, goes for the back line | Artillery, DPS | Chaff Clear, Tanks |
| **Artillery** | Long-range splash | Slow, bunched-up units | Flankers, air |
| **Anti-Air** | Strong against air, can still hit ground | Aerial | Ground units |
| **Aerial** | Fliers, bombers and transports | Ground armies without anti-air | Anti-Air |
| **Titan** (tier 4) | One huge, very tough unit | Most things in a straight fight | Single Target DPS, big chaff swarms |

> **Tip:** The table shows each role's intent. The real result depends on the exact numbers, so check a unit's **Good against · bad against** list, which comes from measured battles.

## How the counters were measured

Every unit's page lists the rival units it beats most often and the ones it loses to. The numbers come from the game's own battle simulation: an army of one unit type fights an army of another, at equal credits, again and again. The [counter web](#/counters) shows every pairing at once.

- Results are for armies of a single unit type, with no techs, levels, items or spells. A mixed army can cover a weakness.
- The Hard AI uses the same table to pick its counters (see [The AI](#/page/ai)).
- The table is measured again after balance changes, so a few results may be older than the latest numbers.
