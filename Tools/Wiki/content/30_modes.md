## The main menu

Each launch opens with a disclaimer; click anywhere to go on. The main menu has **Play**, **Settings** and **Quit**, with the game's version in the lower left. Behind it plays a battle of vehicles; **Settings → Menu background → Classic** switches to the older menu scene.

## The Play page

| Mode | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Faction mode** | The full game: the 5-card faction draft, a faction and sub-faction, towers and cards. Choose a **difficulty** (Easy, Normal, Hard). Base health is 2,000, moved up or down by your starting card. |
| **Unlock Skirmish** | No factions: any unit can be unlocked (one a round, at the usual unlock prices), and every spell and card of all three factions can appear, each once. You get 5 starting cards of 2 random tier 1 squads, and towers as usual. Choose a **difficulty**, a **base health** from 100 to 10,000, and which **cards** appear: all, none of the reinforcement cards, or only reinforcement cards. |
| **Tutorial** | A guided, scripted match. |
| **Sandbox** | Build both armies freely and watch them fight. |

Your difficulty is remembered between matches (Normal at first). See [The AI](#/page/ai) for what each one does.

## The Tutorial

A fixed match: you play **UNSC Noble**, starting with two Marine squads, against a scripted **Banished** army that grows each round and never takes cards. Bases start at 600 health.

It has **30 steps**: the starting card, the camera, the top bar, unlocking and buying, squad and shop stats, moving and Undo, both towers and what they sell, the battle and its speeds, the result, cards, spells, levels, techs, items, flank zones, exclusive units, finishing the match, and Battle stats.

Each step shows a panel and a pulsing outline on what it points at. It moves on when you've done the action, or when you press Next. **Ready stays greyed** until a step allows the battle. **Skip tutorial** returns to the menu. If you follow along, you win in about round 5.

## The Sandbox

- Pick units from **every** faction and sub-faction for either side (switch between Blue and Red), and place them anywhere in both zones. There are **no credits and no limits**, and every squad can be moved.
- Set **levels and techs** freely. **Every item** is free (one per squad). There are no spells.
- **Run battle** fights it out, and **Reset** puts everything back where it was placed.
- Also: copy a side, clear a side, a **Towers** switch (off at first), a **Map** button, the battle seed and speed controls.

Battles in the Sandbox play out exactly like match battles, which makes it the place to test a matchup.

## In a match

- **The deployment screen** has your credits and next income, squads bought (x of 2), whether you've used the round's unlock, both bases, the shop (by tier, then price, with sub-faction exclusives marked), the squad stats window, the spell bar, the inventory, the tower buttons, **Ready** and **Undo**.
- Clicking a unit in the shop shows a level 1 squad's stats before you buy.
- **Camera:** W/A/S/D, the arrow keys or the screen edges to pan; the wheel to zoom; hold the right mouse button and drag to turn and tilt; **C** resets the view; **K** turns camera shake on or off.
- **Pause menu** (Esc): Resume, Settings, Quit to Main Menu, Quit Game.
- **Battle stats** (at the end of a match): a tab per battle, with the field from above as it started and both armies listed by damage **dealt**, **taken**, **stopped** (by armor, shields and arcs) and bodies that lived.
- **Battle log:** each game writes a log file, which Settings can turn off. **Open battle logs** opens the folder.

## Settings

- **Sound:** master, music, effects and interface volumes.
- **Display:** screen, windowed or full screen, resolution, quality, VSync.
- **Interface and effects:** UI scale, camera shake, frame rate counter, battle log, menu background.
- **Reset to defaults.** The same panel opens from the pause menu.
