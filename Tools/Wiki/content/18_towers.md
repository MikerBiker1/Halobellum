## Two towers

Each side has a **Command Center** on its left and a **Research Center** on its right, in the **middle of its zone** by depth. Each takes up 3 × 3 squares.

- A tower is a **ground target like any other**: the closest enemy is the closest enemy. It has **Heavy armor** at every level and a 3 m collision radius.
- It **fires nothing**, can't be infected or seeded, and **isn't part of the army**: it doesn't keep a battle going, deals no base damage and is never a buff target.
- Both towers are rebuilt at full health for every battle. Their **levels last for the match**.
- The two behave the same in battle. They differ only in what they sell: see [Economy](#/page/economy) for the Command Center's and the Research Center's shops.

## Health and levels

A tower's health doubles with every level, and its shockwave gets shorter. A level-up costs **100 credits**, from either tower's panel, up to level 5. The **XP** column is what the squad that destroys it earns.

{{tower-levels}}

The enemy sees your tower levels as they were at the start of the last battle.

## The shockwave

When a tower falls, **its own side's whole army** suffers a shockwave:

{{shockwave}}

- It lasts **10, 8, 6, 4 or 2 seconds**, depending on the fallen tower's level.
- If the second tower falls while a shockwave is still running, its time is **added**; the effect doesn't get stronger.
- It also weakens seeds planted by that side, and stretches beam charges and wind-ups.
- A side under a shockwave pulses pale blue, and a bar under its panel shows the time left.

> **Tip:** Higher tower levels make a tower harder to reach and cut the shockwave short. The Hard AI levels a tower that has been flanked up to level 3 and puts anti-air beside it.
