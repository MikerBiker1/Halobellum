## Credits and income

You start round 1 with **200 credits**. After every round that doesn't end the match, both players are paid. Income starts at 200 and rises by 200 each round, up to a **cap of 1,600**. Unspent credits carry over with no limit.

{{income-table}}

- **Economy cards** pay their bonus **on top of** the 1,600 cap.
- **Rapid Resupply** (from the Command Center) takes 300 off your next income.

## Unlocking unit types

- A unit type must be **unlocked once** before you can buy it. It stays unlocked for the match.
- **At most 1 unlock per round**, even for free tier 1 units.
- You can only unlock units in your roster: your faction's base roster plus your sub-faction's exclusives.
- Unlock prices: tier 1 **free**, tier 2 **50**, tier 3 about **half the squad's price** (200 to 250), Titans **350**. Each unit page shows its exact price.
- You can unlock and buy a unit in the same round.
- Reinforcement cards unlock their unit for free and ignore the 1-unlock limit.

## Buying squads

- Buying a unit deploys a **squad**: a fixed number of bodies, from 1 up to 20 or more.
- Typical prices per squad: tier 1 100 to 200, tier 2 200 to 300, tier 3 400 to 500, Titans 800.
- You can buy **2 new squads per round**, of any types. To buy more:
  - the Command Center's **extra slot**: +1 for this round, 50 credits, once per round;
  - the **Additional Deployment Slot** card: +1 every round for the rest of the match.
- Free draft squads and reinforcement squads never count toward the limit.
- If your zone has no free spot, you can't buy.

## Selling

| Squad | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Bought this round, hasn't fought yet | **100%** of what you paid (level-ups included), and the purchase slot comes back |
| Any other bought squad | **50%** of its price plus level-up spending |
| Free draft squad | Always **50%** of its cost, plus 50% of level-ups |
| Reinforcement squad, before its first battle | What you paid for the card's squads (a free card gives 0) |
| Reinforcement squad, after a battle | 50% of its worth plus 50% of level-ups |

Selling a squad that carries an item puts the item back in your Inventory.

## Spending on what you have

- **Level-ups** cost half the squad's base price, at every level. See [Levels and experience](#/page/levels).
- **Techs** cost 100 to 300 credits each, bought once per unit type. See [Techs](#/page/techs).

## What the towers sell

Both sides have two towers in their zone (see [Towers](#/page/towers)). Their shops are open during deployment in Faction mode, Unlock Skirmish and the tutorial.

### Command Center

Each purchase can be made once per round.

| Purchase | Cost | Effect |
| --- | --: | --- |
| Level up the tower | 100 | +1 level (max 5): double health, shorter shockwave |
| Extra squad slot | 50 | One more squad can be bought this round |
| Oil Zone | 150 | A one-use offensive spell: a line that slows ground units and makes fire hurt more |
| Rapid Resupply | free | **+200 credits now, 300 less income next round** |
| Elite Recruitment | 100 | Squads **bought** this round start at **level 2**, for half their base price more (1.5× the price for 2× health and damage) |

### Research Center

| Purchase | Cost | Effect |
| --- | --: | --- |
| Level up the tower | 100 | As above |
| Health research, step 1 / step 2 | 100 / 300 | Whole army **+15%** health, then **+30%** in all |
| Damage research, step 1 / step 2 | 100 / 300 | Whole army **+12%** damage, then **+24%** in all |

Research is open to every faction and to the AI. It lasts for the match and also reaches what your units drop, release or spawn, and their death bursts.

## Skip bonuses

- Skipping a **general** card offer: **+50** credits.
- Skipping a **reinforcement** offer: **+100** credits.
