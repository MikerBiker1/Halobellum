## What kind of game

Halobellum is a single-player 3D **auto battler** played against an AI opponent. Each round you spend credits on squads, unlocks, upgrades and cards, place your army on your half of the field, then press **Ready**. The battle then plays itself: you can't give orders, only pause, change the speed, move the camera and inspect units.

The game is about **counterplay**. Read the enemy's army, buy what beats it, and place it well. Every unit attacks the closest enemy it's allowed to hit, so cheap bodies that soak up fire stay useful all game.

## How a match is won

- Each side has a **base** with **2,000 health** (your starting card moves this up or down a little).
- After every battle, **every body still alive on both sides** hits the enemy base for its **base damage**. Both bases are hit at the same moment.
- A base at 0 or below loses. If both fall in the same round, the side further below zero loses. An exact tie undoes that round's damage and the match goes on.
- There is no round limit: the match ends when a base falls.

> **Tip:** Losing a battle carries no extra penalty. What counts is how many of your bodies survive, and how much each is worth. A wiped-out army deals nothing; a few expensive survivors can deal a lot.

## A match at a glance

1. **Draft** (once): pick one of 5 starting cards. Each gives a faction, a sub-faction and 2 free starting squads. See [Match flow](#/page/match-flow).
2. **Cards** (from round 2): take one of 3 cards (a spell, an item, an economy card, a buff, training or reinforcements), or skip it for credits. See [Cards, spells and items](#/page/cards).
3. **Deployment**: unlock unit types, buy and place squads, level them up, buy techs, use your towers. There's no timer. See [Economy](#/page/economy) and [The battlefield](#/page/battlefield).
4. **Battle**: up to 90 seconds of automatic fighting. See [Combat](#/page/combat).
5. **Result**: survivors damage the enemy base, squads earn experience, both sides get income. Then the next round starts.
