## When cards are offered

- **From round 2**, every round starts with a card offer, with the battlefield visible behind it.
- **Reinforcement offers** come in rounds **4, 6, 8…** (every other round from round 4). The other rounds (2, 3, 5, 7, 9…) offer **general cards**.
- Each offer shows up to **3 cards**. Take one and pay its price, or **skip** for **50** credits (general) or **100** (reinforcement).
- **Hide cards** puts them aside so you can look over the field before choosing.

## Kinds of card

| Kind | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Spell** | A placed, recharging spell you keep for the match |
| **Item** | Goes to your Inventory, then onto one squad for the match |
| **Economy** | More credits every round, paid on top of the income cap |
| **Buff** | A lasting bonus for the whole army, or +1 squad per round |
| **Training** | Intensive Training: fills one squad's XP bar |
| **Reinforcement** | Squads of one unit type at a set level |

- General offers draw at random from your faction's spells plus the 15 shared cards, among those **allowed by now** (each card has a first round) and that you don't already own the most copies of.
- The +1 squad buff comes from round 5. The three strongest buffs, the larger economy card and each faction's 350-credit superweapon come from round 7.
- Items can be taken any number of times; most spells 1 or 2 times; Intensive Training twice; economy and buff cards once.

See the cards themselves on [Spells](#/spells) and [Items & cards](#/cards).

## Spells

- A spell is **bought once**; using it is free. A spell taken this round can be used this round.
- It's **placed during deployment** and takes effect after its own delay once the battle starts (0 to 6 s; the superweapons take 6 s).
- **Offensive** spells (red outline) can go **anywhere on the field** except the flank strips. Damage spells and EMPs **also hit your own units** (never your towers). Smoke and oil affect both sides.
- **Defensive** spells and buffs (blue outline) can only go **in your own zone**, and only help you.
- Placed spells are hidden from the opponent until the battle starts. Taking one back before Ready costs nothing.
- **Cooldown:** a spell used in round R can be used again in round **R + cooldown + 1**, so cooldown 1 means every other round. Each copy recharges on its own.
- Line-shaped spells take two clicks: one end, then aim the other.
- Kills by spells give no XP.

### What spells do

- **Damage:** one strike in a circle, several strikes over time, **scattered strikes** (small blasts at random spots inside a wide circle), or a **sweeping beam** along a line. Damage uses the normal [damage table](#/page/combat) and the spell's target filter. Some Flood spells make the Unarmored enemies they kill rise as infected units.
- **Buff:** your units inside the circle **when it goes off** get a bonus shield worth a share of their health, and/or a damage bonus, for the rest of the battle.
- **Drop squad:** one or more squads land at the spot, for that battle only.
- **Disrupt (EMP):** strips shields, switches off techs, slows **armored** (Light and Heavy) units by 40%, and cancels wind-ups in its area, on both sides.
- **Smoke:** an 8 × 45 m line. Every unit inside, on both sides and air included, has its weapon range **halved** for the rest of the battle.

## Oil Zone

Bought from the Command Center for 150 credits, once per round, it becomes a **single-use offensive spell**. It's an **8 × 45 m line** placed like smoke. For **15 s** from the start of the battle, **ground** units of both sides inside it move **40% slower** and take **1.5× fire damage**.

## Items

- Taken items go to your **Inventory**. During deployment, drag one onto the **hexagon** over a squad (or click the item, then the hexagon).
- **One item per squad.** It stays on that squad for the match and helps every body in it.
- Selling the squad returns the item to the Inventory.
- Items can give health, damage (death bursts too), move speed, range, lifesteal or healing, or let the squad be moved every round.
- The enemy only sees an item once a battle has revealed it.

## Intensive Training

Fills one squad's XP bar so it can level up now. It recharges for **1 round** (usable every other round), and you can hold up to **2 copies**. It refuses a squad whose bar is already full or that is at the top level, so it can't be wasted.

## Reinforcement cards

- Offered from round **4**, then every other round. Each of the 3 cards is a **different random unit type** from your roster, tier 3 at most (no Titans).
- Each card has a random **level**: level 1 up to round 4, up to level 2 in rounds 5 to 8, up to level 3 from round 9.
- The round's **budget** is **200 + 50 × the round number** (round 4: 400, round 6: 500, round 10: 700).
- A squad's worth is its base price plus half its base price for each level above 1. Squads are added until their total worth reaches or passes the budget, and you pay **the total minus the budget** (0 if it lands exactly on budget). For example, with a budget of 400, a level 1 squad worth 200 becomes 2 squads, free.
- Taking the card **unlocks the unit**, ignoring the 1-unlock limit. Its squads appear near the middle of your zone, can be moved this round, **don't count** toward the squads-per-round limit, and start with 0 XP. If your zone is full, extra squads are lost.
