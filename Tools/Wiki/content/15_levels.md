## Squads and bodies

A **squad** is what you buy and place. It's made of **bodies**, the individual units. Health and damage are per body; the price is per squad. A Flood **pack** is one body that stands for 6 tiny forms.

## Earning experience

- Squads earn experience (XP) from **kills** and the **damage** that led to them. Each enemy body killed is worth its squad's price ÷ squad size, **+50% for each level it had above 1**.
- **The XP of a kill is split:** **50%** goes to the squad that lands the killing blow, and the other **50%** is shared by every squad that damaged the body, by the share of the damage each dealt (the killer included). Shield damage counts; overkill doesn't.
- **Example:** a Spartan squad deals 90% of a body's damage and a Scorpion finishes it with the last 10%. The Scorpion gets 50% + 5% = **55%** of its XP, the Spartan 45%. Before the split, the Scorpion got all of it.
- Nothing is lost or added: a battle gives out the same XP in all, only shared out differently. Squads that soften targets level sooner; squads that mostly finish them off level later.
- Kills by splash, turrets and death bursts count, even a blast the unit dies in (the Infected Pelican's dive): its squad comes back next round with the XP. A Seeder earns XP for bodies its seeds kill.
- **What a squad brings into the battle earns for it:** kills by the bodies it drops (the Infected Phantom's Combat Forms), releases (a Carrier Form's or a Spawner's forms, the Pelican's crash) or raises (infected and seeded hosts) count as its own, and so does the damage they deal.
- **What a squad brings fights at its level:** its drops, releases, death-burst spawns and seed forms have the squad's level (its health and damage), but not its techs or item. Risen infected bodies are the victim's own body and keep the victim's stats. Spell squads stay level 1. A levelled summoned body is worth more XP when killed, like any body.
- Destroying a tower gives the tower's XP for its level (see [Towers](#/page/towers)), split the same way: 50% to the squad that lands the last hit, the rest to the squads that damaged it, by damage.
- **No XP** for kills by spells or by squads a spell brought, or for killing generic risen infected bodies. Spell damage is left out of the split, and a kill that gives no XP gives none to the squads that damaged the body either.
- **The bar fills during the battle:** select a squad and watch it rise with each kill it lands or helped with. The XP is its own from then on; it can level up once the battle is over.
- **The XP bar is 2× the squad's base price**, at every level. XP stops at a full bar (nothing carries over) and goes back to 0 on a level-up. A squad at the top level earns no more.
- A squad that can level up shows a pulsing yellow arrow, and the round result window lists it.
- **Tech icons:** during deployment each squad shows a row of small icons above it, one per tech its type has: a reticle for range, three bullets for rate of fire, a bullet through a wall for damage, an armour plate for health, running legs for speed, a dome for a shield, a blast for splash, a flame for a wider cone, an aircraft for anti-air, troops coming down for a drop, a bullet and a plus for an extra shot, a clock for reload, a gear for anything else. Hover one for its name. The enemy's squads show the techs they had at the last battle, never what it has bought since. The icons go when the battle starts.

## Levelling up

- With a full bar, pay **half the squad's base price** during deployment to raise its level. The price is the same at every level.
- **Level all** levels every ready squad of that unit type at once (shown when 2 or more are ready).
- **The top level is 5.**
- A squad at level L has **L × its base health and L × its weapon damage** (death bursts included):

| Level | Health and damage | Gain over the level before |
| --- | --: | --: |
| 1 | ×1 | – |
| 2 | ×2 | +100% |
| 3 | ×3 | +50% |
| 4 | ×4 | +33% |
| 5 | ×5 | +25% |

- Shields, speed and base damage don't change with level.
- Squads keep their level and XP between rounds. The opponent sees a squad's level once a battle has revealed it.

> **Tip:** Two shortcuts: **Intensive Training** (a card) fills a squad's XP bar at once, and the Command Center's **Elite Recruitment** makes this round's new squads start at level 2.
