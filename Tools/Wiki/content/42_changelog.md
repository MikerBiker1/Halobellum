Every version since version numbers began (0.378.0). The first number is the owner's, the second goes up for a substantial change and the third for a small one. The D numbers point to the full entries in the project's decision log.

## 0.389.3 · 2026-11-09
- **Manual:** this changelog.

## 0.389.2 · 2026-11-09
- **Manual:** a Balance section on [The AI](#/page/ai) page with the Insane tournament numbers.

## 0.389.1 · 2026-11-09
- **Spawner:** 3,600 health (4,000 in 0.389.0). Covenant-based Flood now wins 50% Insane against Insane. (D125)

## 0.389.0 · 2026-11-09
- **Balance is now measured with the Insane AI against itself.** Before tuning it ranged from 37% (Noble) to 60% (UNSC-based Flood); now every sub-faction wins 49% to 51%. No prices changed. (D125)
- **Weaker:** Pure Form Ranged shards 60 → 48; Brute Grenadier grenades 34 → 31; Sparrowhawk autocannon 18 → 13 and health 550 → 480; Longsword bombs 150 → 135; Vulture health 900 → 650 and missiles 50 → 44; Condor Gunship health 1,500 → 1,150; Infested Hornet rockets 18 → 15 and spore bombs 36 → 29; Infested Scorpion cannon 80 → 72; Infested Marine scythe 20 → 18; Pelican autocannon 14 → 10.
- **Stronger:** Elephant main cannon 220 → 300; Warthog health 300 → 360; Spartan Heavy Weapons health 1,700 → 2,400 and chaingun 11 → 14; Spartan Fireteam health 420 → 500; Grizzly health 2,100 → 2,400 and cannons 100 → 110; Flood Abomination health 8,500 → 10,000; Infected Phantom health 1,100 → 1,250; Carrier Form health 140 → 170; Infested Ghost health 270 → 330; Infested Elite health 420 → 500 and claw 42 → 46; Heavy Grunts needles 6.5 → 8; Grunt Goblin health 1,500 → 1,750 and cannons 9 → 10.
- **Spawner:** Spore Lash 40 → 55, and it releases three Infection Forms and five Combat Forms in turn (two and two), still every 4 seconds. Swarm clearers such as the Flamethrower Trooper are its counter.

## 0.388.1 · 2026-11-08
- **Manual:** losing a battle also gives the enemy XP.

## 0.388.0 · 2026-11-08
- **This manual:** a web wiki built from the game's own data, with every unit, tech, spell, card and item, the counter web, a compare page and search. (D124)

## 0.387.0 · 2026-11-08
- **Insane difficulty:** a fourth AI above Hard, built from the owner's own winning games. It learns from the last battle, answers air at once, feeds its best units, and places its squads better. It beat Hard in about 94% of matches. (D123)

## 0.386.6 · 2026-11-08
- **Flood aircraft fall:** the Infested Banshee and Infested Hornet fall burning like the aircraft they were.
- **Shockwave:** its line now has a bar that runs down with the time left.
- **Seeded machines:** a machine the Flood takes over no longer explodes or falls; the Flood's growths swell on it for 2 seconds and then it turns. (D122)

## 0.386.5 · 2026-11-08
- **Ghost:** its blast is heard at any battle speed. The Infested Ghost drifts and blows up too, in Flood gore. A battle's last crash is still heard after it ends. (D121)

## 0.386.4 · 2026-11-08
- **Sounds:** a new fall-and-crash sound for the Banshee and Infested Banshee; a turret explosion for every machine that had a generated death; a knocked-out Ghost drifts for 2.1 seconds, then blows up blue. (D120)

## 0.386.3 · 2026-11-08
- **Taken vehicles** use their original vehicle's sounds.
- **Spells:** clicking a spell's name shows what it does, when it strikes and its recharge.
- **Towers** have heavy armor. (D119)

## 0.386.2 · 2026-11-08
- **Sounds:** every UNSC rocket, missile and bomb is heard coming down until it lands. (D118)

## 0.386.1 · 2026-11-08
- **Sounds:** the SMG and the Ghost sound every shot; a falling aircraft is heard diving, then crashing; new Longsword bomb sounds. (D117)

## 0.386.0 · 2026-11-08
- **No Infested Grunt** (it doesn't fit Halo lore).
- **Spawner:** releases Combat Forms in turn with its Infection Forms, instead of an Infested Elite.
- **Prices:** Sparrowhawk 250, Skitterer 125. Every sub-faction 48% to 52% under Hard. (D116)

## 0.385.0 · 2026-11-08
- **Units with nothing to shoot** use their other guns, then walk to the closest enemy and wait there (aircraft most often).
- Level 1 is shown in unit stats; the Spawner's Swarm Form rises from the ground; the Swarm Form gets a Long Range tech. (D115)

## 0.384.0 · 2026-11-08
- **A fourth tech slot.** The Grizzly's Double Shot fires 2 shells per attack; most gun units can buy a flat +8 m range tech.
- **Swarm Brood** hatches one Swarm Form; the Condor's lasers fire staggered; machine guns and autocannons sound every shot. Sparrowhawk 275. (D114)

## 0.383.3 · 2026-11-08

## 0.383.2 · 2026-11-08

## 0.383.1 · 2026-11-08
