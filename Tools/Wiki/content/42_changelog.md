Every version since version numbers began (0.378.0). The first number is the owner's, the second goes up for a substantial change and the third for a small one. The D numbers point to the full entries in the project's decision log.

## 0.398.1 · 2026-11-12
- **No change to the game.** New balance tools: a unit intent sheet (what each unit is for and how strong it should be for its price) and the army slot test, which measures what each unit adds to the armies the AI really builds. Balance now goes unit by unit. (D137)

## 0.398.0 · 2026-11-12
- **The XP bar fills during the battle:** select a squad and watch it rise as it kills. (D136)
- **What a squad brings into the battle earns XP for it:** kills by the bodies it drops, releases or raises count as its own, so a Carrier Form earns for its Infection Forms, a Spawner for its broods, an Infected Phantom for its Combat Forms, a Seeder for the hosts it took. Squads a spell brings still earn nothing. Insane against Insane, every sub-faction wins 47% to 53% (Covenant-based Flood 50% → 51%). (D136)
- **The Infected Pelican's dive** already earned XP; now it also does when its blast ends the battle, and what a blast kills as the battle ends is dead, no longer left standing as survivors. (D136)

## 0.397.1 · 2026-11-12
- **Spartan Grenadier:** carries its rocket launcher on the shoulder when walking, like the Spartan. (D135)

## 0.397.0 · 2026-11-12
- **Infected Pelican:** tougher and harder-hitting, so it reaches the enemy before it is shot down: health 1,350 (was 900), blast 1,200 (900). Every sub-faction still wins 48% to 52% Insane against Insane; Covenant-based Flood 49% → 50%, UNSC-based Flood unchanged at 48%. (D134)
- **The counter test** accepts 70% for the Hunter Captain against the Flood Abomination: no other unit beats the Abomination at equal credits. (D134)

## 0.396.0 · 2026-11-11
- **Spartan CQB:** its shotgun can't reach aircraft any more, so it shoots ground only, and is much stronger for it: 300 credits (was 400), unlock 150 (200), shotgun 120 (80), shield 600 (450). (D133)
- **Spartan Heavy Weapons:** 400 credits (was 450), unlock 200 (225); its chaingun 18 (14) and reaching 20 m (15), as far as the rockets did. Noble had fallen to 45% after the Heavy lost its rockets; every sub-faction now wins 48% to 52% Insane against Insane. (D133)

## 0.395.0 · 2026-11-11
- **Spartan Heavy Weapons:** your machine gun, carried like a minigun and fired from the hip. It no longer fires rockets. (D132)

## 0.394.0 · 2026-11-11
- **Zealot:** your twinblade, its blades glowing white in a cyan haze. (D131)
- **Marines and Infested Marines:** your MA5B. (D131)
- **Spartan Fireteam:** your battle rifle, in place of the assault rifle. (D131)

## 0.393.2 · 2026-11-11
- **The AI** knows the Spartan CQB has no leap: its counter numbers were measured again (it now beats 20 of 68 units one on one, against 21). (D130)

## 0.393.1 · 2026-11-11
- **Spartan CQB:** no more thruster leap. It runs up to its shotgun's range and fires. (D130)

## 0.393.0 · 2026-11-11
- **New animations for the Spartans, the Elite and the Zealot.** They used the Marines' walk and shot. Now the Spartan and Heavy Weapons walk with the big weapon on the shoulder and stand to fire it shouldered; the Fireteam, Grenadier and Sniper walk with the weapon low in front and shoulder it to aim; the CQB runs and fires with the shotgun at the hip; the Elite carries its plasma rifle at the hip in its right hand and fires with the arm straight out; the Zealot runs with its sword pointing down behind it. Each shot gives a small kick, and the walks match each unit's speed. (D129)

## 0.392.0 · 2026-11-11
- **Insane in Unlock Skirmish rushes a tower.** It sends its whole army up one lane at one of your towers and levels its other tower, so you lose time there. It now beats Hard about 63% of the time in skirmish, up from 44%. Insane in Faction mode plays as before. (D128)

## 0.391.0 · 2026-11-10
- **Seeder seeds:** a seed now only eats a host the Flood can turn into one of its own (Marines, Warthogs, Scorpions, Hornets, Elites, Ghosts, Banshees, Wraiths, Pelicans, Phantoms) or an aircraft. Anything else, such as a Spartan or a Hunter, is only slowed. Every sub-faction wins 49% to 51% Insane against Insane. (D127)

## 0.390.0 · 2026-11-10
- **Seeders shoot what they can't seed.** With nothing it can seed in range, a Seeder now attacks the closest enemy anyway: an Elephant, a Spartan with its shield up, a Flood unit. It still seeds first when it can. (D127)
- **Seeder:** its death is the Infection Form leap sound.

## 0.389.3 · 2026-11-09

## 0.389.2 · 2026-11-09

## 0.389.1 · 2026-11-09
- **Spawner:** 3,600 health (4,000 in 0.389.0). Covenant-based Flood now wins 50% Insane against Insane. (D125)

## 0.389.0 · 2026-11-09
- **Balance is now measured with the Insane AI against itself.** Before tuning it ranged from 37% (Noble) to 60% (UNSC-based Flood); now every sub-faction wins 49% to 51%. No prices changed. (D125)
- **Weaker:** Pure Form Ranged shards 60 → 48; Brute Grenadier grenades 34 → 31; Sparrowhawk autocannon 18 → 13 and health 550 → 480; Longsword bombs 150 → 135; Vulture health 900 → 650 and missiles 50 → 44; Condor Gunship health 1,500 → 1,150; Infested Hornet rockets 18 → 15 and spore bombs 36 → 29; Infested Scorpion cannon 80 → 72; Infested Marine scythe 20 → 18; Pelican autocannon 14 → 10.
- **Stronger:** Elephant main cannon 220 → 300; Warthog health 300 → 360; Spartan Heavy Weapons health 1,700 → 2,400 and chaingun 11 → 14; Spartan Fireteam health 420 → 500; Grizzly health 2,100 → 2,400 and cannons 100 → 110; Flood Abomination health 8,500 → 10,000; Infected Phantom health 1,100 → 1,250; Carrier Form health 140 → 170; Infested Ghost health 270 → 330; Infested Elite health 420 → 500 and claw 42 → 46; Heavy Grunts needles 6.5 → 8; Grunt Goblin health 1,500 → 1,750 and cannons 9 → 10.
- **Spawner:** Spore Lash 40 → 55, and it releases three Infection Forms and five Combat Forms in turn (two and two), still every 4 seconds. Swarm clearers such as the Flamethrower Trooper are its counter.

## 0.388.1 · 2026-11-08

## 0.388.0 · 2026-11-08

## 0.387.0 · 2026-11-08
