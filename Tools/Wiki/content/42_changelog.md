Every version since version numbers began (0.378.0). The first number is the owner's, the second goes up for a substantial change and the third for a small one. The D numbers point to the full entries in the project's decision log.

## 0.418.0 · 2026-11-24
- **Rhino:** your new model, its turret turning on its round base. (D184)
- **Hornet and Infested Hornet:** your new Hornet, its two rotors spinning. (D184)

## 0.417.0 · 2026-11-24
- **LAN Game:** a new main menu item to play one against one with another person on the same local network. One player hosts (Faction mode or Unlock Skirmish) and the other joins from the list of games found or by the address the host's screen shows. Both deploy at the same time and the battle plays on both screens; each sees their own army at the bottom. The two games send only decisions and check they agree before every battle. No pause during a LAN battle; leaving forfeits. See the LAN Game page. (D182)

## 0.416.2 · 2026-11-24
- **Heavy Grunts health:** each Heavy Grunt has 130 health (was 150). Unggoy Rebellion was the sub-faction furthest from 50%. Insane against Insane at 2000 health over five seeds (18,000 matches): Unggoy Rebellion 50.0% on average (was 51.2%), every sub-faction 49.2% to 50.8%, every faction 49.7% to 50.2%. (D181)

## 0.416.1 · 2026-11-23
- **Infested Banshee and Rhino health:** the Infested Banshee has 440 health (was 400) and the Rhino 1100 (was 1000). Insane against Insane at 2000 health over five seeds (18,000 matches): every sub-faction 49.2% to 51.2% on average, every faction 49.5% to 50.5%. (D180)

## 0.416.0 · 2026-11-23
- **Pelican and Covenant Phantom health:** the Pelican has 900 health (was 1400) and its autocannon deals 32 a shot (was 25); the Covenant Phantom has 1000 (was 1550) and its turrets deal 20 (was 15), in line with other 300-credit units. The AI's counter table measured the two again: the Pelican now loses to Warthogs, Ghosts, Choppers, Marauders and Spartan Snipers. (D179)
- **Sub-factions brought back into the band:** Gremlin EMP Railgun 46 (was 38), Grizzly Twin Cannons 120 (was 110), Infested Elite Scythe Claw 70 (was 60), Infested Banshee Spore Bomb 42 (was 37), Infested Wraith mortar 98 (was 90), Spawner Spore Lash 62 (was 55), Brute Grenadier grenades 32 (was 35). Insane against Insane at 2000 health (14,400 matches): every sub-faction 48.1% to 52.0%, every faction 49.6% to 50.4%. (D179)

## 0.415.2 · 2026-11-23
- **Transport troops deal no base damage:** the Marines a Pelican drops, the Covenant Phantom's Grunts and an Infected Phantom's Combat Forms no longer hit the enemy base at the round's end, like summoned bodies. The transport itself still does. (D178)

## 0.415.1 · 2026-11-22

## 0.415.0 · 2026-11-22
- **XP paid as damage lands:** 75% of a body's worth goes to the squads that damage it, as the damage lands, even if it survives; 25% goes to the squad that kills it (was half on the kill and half only when it died). A body that heals and is hurt again pays again, so healing and regeneration feed the enemy XP. A body killed from full health gives out the same XP as before. Insane against Insane at 2000 health (7,200 matches): every sub-faction 48.9% to 50.7%, every faction 50.0%. (D177)

## 0.414.2 · 2026-11-22

## 0.414.1 · 2026-11-22
