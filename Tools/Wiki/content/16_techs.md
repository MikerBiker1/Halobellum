## How techs work

- Each unit type has **up to 4 techs**: three, and on many units a fourth that is either a flat **+8 m range** tech for 300 credits or one that changes how the unit plays. Techs cost **100 to 300** credits.
- A tech is bought **once per unit type** and applies to **every squad of that type** for the rest of the match, including squads you buy later and reinforcement squads.
- The unit type must be **unlocked**. There's no limit per round, only credits. Techs are bought during deployment.
- An **EMP** switches a unit's techs off for a while; it then fights on its level, item and buffs alone.

> **Note:** The measured counter web and the Hard AI's counter table use base stats (level 1, no techs), so a tech can flip a matchup.

## What a tech can change

- Health, weapon damage (and death burst), rate of fire, move speed.
- Weapon range, in flat metres. This **never** lengthens melee weapons, a charging unit's bash, or the close-in blade of a unit whose main weapon has a minimum range.
- Shield points and shield regen. A unit without a shield gains one.
- Splash radius (only for weapons that already splash) and cone width.
- Damage against air, or letting the unit's weapons **shoot air** at all.
- Switching on a **mid-battle drop** or hatch, such as a troop bay.
- Extra shots per attack, and the main weapon's reload time.
